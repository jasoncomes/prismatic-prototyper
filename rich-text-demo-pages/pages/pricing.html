Frequently asked

What counts as an instance?

One deployed integration for one customer. Ten customers on the same integration is ten instances.

Is there a free tier?

Development is free and unlimited. You pay when an instance is deployed to production.

Can I change plans?

At any time. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades at the end of the billing period.
