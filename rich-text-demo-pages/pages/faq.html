Questions

Is there a free tier?

Yes, for development. Production instances need a paid plan.

Can I self-host?

Yes, on your own cloud account. The runner and the control plane deploy separately.

What happens to my data?

  • Execution payloads are retained for 30 days
  • Logs are retained for 30 days
  • Credentials are encrypted at rest and never logged

How do I report a problem?

  1. Check the status page
  2. Search the docs
  3. Open a ticket with the execution ID

Tip: the execution ID is on every test run, under the trigger output.
