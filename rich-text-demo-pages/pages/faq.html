Frequently asked questions

Answers to what we are asked most. If yours is not here, the documentation search is usually faster than a ticket.

Plans and billing

Is there a free tier?

Development is free and unlimited. Production instances need a paid plan.

What counts as an instance?

One deployed integration for one customer. The same integration deployed to ten customers is ten instances.

Can I change plans mid-cycle?

Yes. Upgrades apply immediately and are prorated; downgrades apply at the period end.

Deployment

Can I self-host?

Yes, on your own cloud account. The control plane and the execution runner deploy separately, and both are supported in a single-tenant configuration.

Which regions are available?

  • us-east-1
  • us-west-2
  • eu-west-1
  • ap-southeast-2

Data handling

What happens to my data?

  • Execution payloads are retained for 30 days
  • Logs are retained for 30 days
  • Credentials are encrypted at rest and never written to logs

Can I shorten retention?

Yes, on request. It can be set per organization down to 24 hours.

Getting help

How do I report a problem?

  1. Check the status page
  2. Search the documentation
  3. Open a ticket with the execution identifier

The execution identifier appears on every test run, under the trigger output. Including it turns a day of back and forth into one reply.
